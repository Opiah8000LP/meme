ICONS FOLDER
Put your custom tab icons here. One per section, named after the section id:
  home.svg  identity.svg  personality.svg  worldview.svg  interests.svg
  creative.svg  growth.svg  social.svg  future.svg  years.svg  facts.svg
SVG is best (use one solid colour; the site recolours/inverts it for you). PNG works too, but then change .svg to .png in data/site.js.
Missing icon? No problem, the orb shows two letters instead. Nobody gets hurt.
